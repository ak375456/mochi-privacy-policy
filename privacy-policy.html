01

A little about Mochi

Mochi: Screen & Notch Pets (“Mochi”) is an Android screen-companion app developed by Aftab Fazal. It lets you add animated walking buddies, hanging characters and camera-notch decorations. You can adjust each buddy, create a hanging character from your own image, and optionally link a buddy to an app or phone number.

This version of Mochi has no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or Mochi account system. It does not sell, rent or trade your in-app data, and does not upload your images, contacts, shortcuts or settings to the developer. Mochi Creator is an optional one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The app does not run a developer-operated backend.

Local editing, Google Play paymentsYour cutouts and buddy settings are processed on your device. Creator purchases, Android backup, a phone call, an external link or a message you send us can involve separate services; those situations are explained below.

03

Permissions, explained

Android controls permissions. You can review or revoke them in your device's app settings.

Display over other apps

For screen buddies

Places small, interactive characters above other apps. It is not used to read the underlying screen, capture screenshots or collect what you type.

Foreground service

While buddies run

Keeps user-enabled companions running. Android's foreground-service permissions support this visible feature, with notification controls to stop or temporarily hide buddies.

Notifications

Android 13 and later

Shows Mochi's running status and controls. This permission allows Mochi to display its own notification; it does not give access to your notification inbox.

Phone calls

Optional

Requested only if you choose “Call immediately” for a phone shortcut. The default option opens the dialer for review without this permission. Mochi does not record calls or access your call history.

Image & contact pickers

Your selection only

Android's pickers give access to the image or phone entry you choose. Mochi does not request unrestricted gallery access or permission to read your whole address book. You can type a number instead.

Google Play billing & network

Creator purchase

Lets Mochi display Google's local price, open Google Play's payment screen, check ownership and acknowledge the creator unlock. Google's Billing Library adds Internet and network-state permissions for its billing communication and diagnostics. Payment is handled by Google Play, using its own account and billing systems. These permissions do not give Mochi your payment-card information or upload your photos.

A camera decoration does not use your cameraThe app uses the screen's reported cutout position to place a decoration. No camera, microphone, location or accessibility-service permission is requested.
04

Storage, security & backup

Settings and saved characters are kept in Mochi's private Android app storage. The app relies on Android's application sandbox and your device's security. It does not maintain a cloud copy or offer a cloud-sync account.

A verified creator receipt is stored separately in Android's private no-backup directory. Clearing app data removes this local copy, but does not cancel your Google Play purchase. Use “Restore purchase” with the same purchasing Google account to regain access. Custom images themselves are not restored from Google Play.

Custom cutouts are saved as transparent lossless WebP on Android 11 and later, with PNG used on older supported versions. Editing drafts use private cache, which may be removed when a draft is saved or discarded, when app data is cleared, or by Android. Your original gallery image is not edited or deleted.

Android backup is a separate serviceIf system backup or device transfer is enabled, Android may copy general settings, including character names and selected app shortcuts. Mochi's backup rules exclude saved custom image files and phone-shortcut names and numbers from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Backup behaviour also depends on your Android version, device manufacturer and system settings.

You can manage system backup through Android's settings. Any system backup is handled by your chosen backup provider, not by a Mochi server. See Android's backup documentation for how the platform handles app data.

05

You're in control

Delete a custom buddy

Deleting a custom character removes its saved cutout, settings and linked phone shortcut from the app.

Remove a shortcut

Change or clear the shortcut in that buddy's settings. Removing a phone shortcut deletes the stored name and number for that buddy.

Stop or hide buddies

Use the app or notification controls. Stopping or hiding keeps your saved settings so you can use the same setup later.

Clear everything locally

Use Android's “Clear storage” / “Clear data” option or uninstall Mochi to remove its local files and preferences.

Saved data remains until you change or delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Clearing app data does not delete your original gallery images, another app's data or an existing system backup. Android may restore general settings from a backup when you reinstall, depending on your backup settings.

There is no Mochi online account to delete. Because in-app data is held on your device, the developer cannot retrieve it remotely; local deletion is managed through the app and Android.

What the app is built with

Mochi uses Android and Kotlin libraries for its interface, lifecycle, local editing and background tasks. These are software dependencies, not advertising or analytics services.

Google Play Billing LibraryProduct prices, one-time purchases, ownership checks, purchase restoration and delivery acknowledgement through Google Play

AndroidX support components may also be included through these libraries. Mochi does not integrate ad networks, Firebase Analytics, third-party tracking SDKs or a remote image-processing service.

Gradle and the Android build tools build the app. JUnit, Espresso and Compose testing tools are used for development and verification; they do not provide advertising or user analytics. Library and artwork notices remain subject to their respective licences.

08

When another service is involved

  • Google Play purchases: Google processes the optional Mochi Creator payment and the billing information needed for purchase, fraud prevention, acknowledgement and restoration under Google's privacy policy. Mochi exchanges purchase identifiers with Google Play, stores a verified receipt locally and does not send your photos to Google as part of billing. Google Play may provide the developer with order and transaction records through Play Console; those records are used for purchase support and accounting, not advertising. Refunds are handled through Google Play. A refunded or revoked unlock is removed when a successful ownership check detects it.
  • Opening an app: a shortcut launches the app you selected. That app handles its own permissions and data practices.
  • Making a call: the chosen number is passed to Android's dialer or phone app. Your phone's SIM preference and carrier handle the call; ordinary call charges and the phone app's call history may apply.
  • System pickers: Android and your chosen photo or contact provider manage selection. A provider may use its own cloud service, for example to retrieve a photo; Mochi does not upload your image to it.
  • Contacting us: if you send an email, call or submit a report, we receive the information you choose to share and use it to respond or investigate. Your email or communications provider handles delivery. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
About this GitHub Pages websiteThis policy page has no cookies, scripts, analytics, external fonts or tracking resources of its own. Support messages are separate from the app's local data. We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle your request or meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete it, subject to those obligations.

There are no ads in the current release. The creator unlock includes an ad-free Mochi experience if a later free version introduces ads. Any advertising SDK or change in data practices will require an updated privacy policy and store disclosures before that version is released.
